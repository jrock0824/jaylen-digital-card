About my work

Software and applied AI engineer focused on helping underrepresented niches reduce burnout and improve workflows using Docker, FastAPI, PostgreSQL, and local LLM tooling (Ollama, RAG, guardrails).

As founder of Ezra Intelligence, I build privacy-first AI where sensitive data stays on-premise, aligned to HIPAA, FERPA, and compliance-heavy environments without per-token cloud costs.

Currently working on MAGEN (healthcare security—80% faster investigations, 97%+ detection) and ASA (trauma-informed AI for educators and social workers, validated with 15 users across multiple states).

I'm open to roles blending backend engineering, applied AI, and security operations, such as Software Engineer, AI Engineer, SOC analyst, IAM engineer, or cybersecurity-focused developer.

Building at Ezra Intelligence

Beyond MAGEN and ASA, we're developing vertically focused, data-sovereign AI products across healthcare, education, legal, compliance, and enterprise, 20+ concepts in the pipeline. Tap a product to see what it does.

  • MedChart AI · Private clinical documentation

    HIPAA-aligned ambient scribe for clinics, local transcription, SOAP notes, and ICD-10 coding without PHI leaving the facility network.

  • SovereignBot · White label private AI builder

    No-code toolkit for businesses to deploy custom assistants on their own knowledge bases using local Ollama inference, private ChatGPT for your company.

  • ScribeDesk · Government & compliance document AI

  • TutorMind & EduAuthor · School deployed learning AI

    District-hosted tutoring and curriculum tools, Socratic homework help, lesson plans, and teacher dashboards with student data kept on school servers (FERPA aware).

  • CodePilot Local · Private AI coding assistant

    IDE-integrated autocomplete and repo-aware RAG for teams that can; send code to cloud copilots, security scanning and test generation included.
